=== Unsupervised Suspicious Review Detection (Isolation Forest) ===
No labelled fake/genuine ground truth exists for this dataset -- this is
anomaly detection over review features, not a fake-review classifier, and
no accuracy figure is reported for that reason.

Reviews analysed: 50,000
Features: is_short_text, capital_ratio, exclamation_count, verified_purchase_int, max_similarity_within_product, reviewer_review_count_log
Contamination (target flagged rate): 10%

Normal: 45,001 (90.0%)
Potentially suspicious: 4,999 (10.0%)
Near-duplicate reviews (similarity >= 0.95): 36
