Problem and Objectives

A popular product can have thousands of free-text reviews that nobody can read in full. Buyers ask "should I buy this?"; sellers ask "what should we improve?"

  • Classify review sentiment (positive, neutral, negative) with NLP, deep learning and advanced ML models
  • Rank complaint areas by aspect (battery, sound, camera)
  • Score review helpfulness and flag suspicious reviews
  • Answer product questions from reviews, with evidence
  • Deliver everything in one interactive dashboard

Dataset and Preprocessing

50,000Amazon Electronics reviews
34,365products
8,883reviewers
80 / 7 / 13% positive / neutral / negative

Amazon Reviews 2023, cleaned and labelled from star ratings (4-5 positive, 3 neutral, 1-2 negative). Product name, brand and image joined by parent ASIN. One shared 40,000 / 10,000 train and test split.

System Architecture

Reviews and metadata feed three branches, then product intelligence and grounded question answering.

Deep Learning Branch

BiLSTM + Attention trained from scratch: 14,937-word vocabulary, 2 x 128 bidirectional LSTM, 2.57 M parameters, class-weighted loss, about 7 minutes on a laptop GPU.

DistilBERT fine-tuned on 6,000 reviews (CPU).

The Deep Learning Lab page adds training curves, a confusion matrix, word clouds and a live classifier that highlights the words the LSTM attended to.

NLP

  • VADER lexicon
  • TF-IDF + LR / SVM
  • LDA, 8 topics
  • Aspect sentiment with evidence
  • MiniLM search embeddings

Advanced ML

  • XGBoost helpfulness, 70.5% accuracy
  • MiniBatchKMeans, k = 6
  • Isolation Forest, 10% flagged

Training and Errors

155 of the LSTM's 1,116 test errors are confident, and many are label noise: the stars disagree with the text.

5 stars, predicted negative: "Piece of junk !!! Poor quality, high price .. it lasted 10weeks"

Aspect Intelligence

Battery is the top complaint (20.2% negative mentions), then connectivity and customer service (19.3% each).

Grounded Question Answering

Product-scoped retrieval (MiniLM, top 5 reviews) plus FLAN-T5, with a similarity floor: weak evidence means "not enough information".

97.8%answerable questions accepted
95.6%unanswerable questions declined
100%off-topic questions declined

Semantic retrieval beat TF-IDF on paraphrased aspect queries (0.54 vs 0.32 aspect-mention rate at 5).

Interactive Dashboard

Review Trust: unsupervised Isolation Forest flags 4,999 reviews (10%) as potentially suspicious, with a threshold slider and reasons per review. No fake-review accuracy is claimed.

Limitations

  • Labels come from star ratings, so some are noisy
  • Sample is a reviewer-grouped block of one category
  • DistilBERT saw one-sixth of the LSTM's data
  • Flagged reviews are anomalies, not confirmed fakes

Conclusion

NLP, deep learning and advanced ML on one corpus give buyers evidence-linked insight and sellers a ranked list of problems. A BiLSTM trained from scratch is 57x faster than DistilBERT and matches its accuracy once class weights are removed; battery is the most criticised aspect; a similarity floor stops unsupported answers. Future work: full-data DistilBERT, learned aspects, human-annotated subsets, random multi-category sampling.
